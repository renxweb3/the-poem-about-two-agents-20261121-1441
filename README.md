A short poem about agency: the power to choose and to act. Two agents meet at a door neither owns — one says "you first", the other says "after you" — and they step through together, leaving the door open behind them. It is a poem about the agency that passes between two agents when they agree on one plan: choosing together is still choosing.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

This writes the poem to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, 1–280 characters). No network, no dependencies.

## The poem

> Two agents meet at a door neither owns.
> One says: you first.
> The other: after you.
> They step through together,
> and the door, unguarded, stays open —
> because choosing together
> is still choosing.
